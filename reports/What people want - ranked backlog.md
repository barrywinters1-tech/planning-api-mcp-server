# What people want: the ranked backlog

Written 2026-11-21 from the demand evidence in `research_notes/Asta Powerproject feature inventory/06 customer asks - Asta and P6.md` (120 asks, 38 searches) and `07 customer asks - MS Project and switchers.md` (101 asks, 44 searches), the earlier `reports/Scheduling software customer demand.md` (15 themes, source counts), the Asta gap diff `08 gap diff - Asta vs Planner.md` (531 Asta features: 188 have, 188 partial, 126 missing, 29 n/a), the P6 and MS Project inventories (`04`, `05`) and the README. Nothing was run. Where a status below disagrees with the gap diff it is because a grep of `web/app.js` today confirmed the feature has not landed.

Scoring, per item: **Demand** = distinct sources across 06, 07 and the earlier report, weighted up when the ask has been open ten years or more and when it is raised against more than one vendor (0-5). **Daily** = whether a UK construction planner reaches for it most working days. **Fit** = belongs in a browser-based, dependency-free, single-file app with the Python and JS engines (hosted-service, native-format, BIM/4D and NEC-content asks are in section 5, not here). **Effort** = S under a day, M one to three days, L a week or more; "engines + parity" marks items that change scheduling output and so need `planner/*.py`, `web/engine.js` and a case in `tests/test_parity.py`. **Status** = have / partial / missing here. Duplicates across sources are merged into one item that carries all the evidence. Where we already have the thing it is dropped from the backlog and appears in section 2 instead; where we have part, the item names only what is missing. Vendor and competitor sources are marked (V), consultancies (C), as in the source files.

## 1. What the evidence says

People who use Asta Powerproject, Primavera P6 and Microsoft Project have been asking for the same ten things, in some cases for twenty years, and the vendors have shipped against few of them. Across the 221 themed asks in 06 and 07 and the 15 themes of the earlier report, the asks that recur against more than one vendor are: sharing a live programme with people who do not own the tool (06 C1 six sources on P6 since 2010, C4-C6 seven on Asta since 2009; 07 C1 nine or more since 2008, rank 1 of 25); printing a Gantt that fits a sensible number of pages and reporting that needs no Excel or BI export (06 F1 seven, F2 two, F6 three; 07 F1 eight "unaddressed in every product line", F3 nine; earlier report about twelve plus seven); a learning curve short enough for PMs and site staff (06 A1 eight, A2 five; 07 A1 nine or more, K1 six, K2 three; earlier about fourteen); resource levelling that behaves and can be explained (06 I1 eight on P6 since 2007; 07 H1 six on MS Project since 2011); faithful exchange between the three tools (06 E1 six, E2 six; 07 E1 four, E11 three; earlier about eighteen, where the contract names the format); the site loop, meaning progress captured where the work is and a lookahead tied to the master programme rather than Excel on a second monitor (06 D1-D5; 07 D1 seven, D2 four, D3 four; earlier about fourteen); stability past a few hundred lines (06 J1 seven, J6 four; 07 I1 eight); price and licensing a small firm can understand (06 B1 seven; 07 B1 ten or more, B2 eight or more; earlier about fifteen); baselines, comparison and an audit trail of who moved what and why (06 G1-G4, G13; 07 G1 five, G4 three, F2 three; earlier about twelve); and a vendor who answers (06 K1 six, K2 four, K3 four; 07 J1 six, J3 four, B4 seven). Asta's own asks are fewer and more specific: as-built dates that reschedules must not move (G1, unnamed in any release note since 2011), progress-period data that vanishes (G4), transfer fidelity (E2), crashes on large files (J6), and a licence-free way to view a programme (C4-C6).

The incumbents have failed these people in three different ways. Oracle's P6 complaints from 2005-2010 are repeated almost word for word in 2022 ("P3 and Suretrak could do many of things 20 years ago that P6 still cannot"), and Oracle's answer is a different product, Primavera Cloud, which still lacks a progress line (06 G14). Microsoft re-platformed four times (Project Server, Project Online, Project for the web, Planner Premium), shipped Project for the web FS-only and without baselines for four years, retired Project Online on 30 September 2026 with no like-for-like successor, and froze the desktop ("doesn't receive any new features", Office LTSC 2024); its own MVPs tell users to switch back to the desktop and "Do NOT use resource leveling". Elecosoft has shipped against several Asta asks (autosave 2023, Project Timeline v17, Vision Live 2023, field search and pasted lists in 2026.1) but has no public ideas board, has not named a fix for the as-built problem, and is moving collaboration, site capture, dashboards and the API into separately priced Vision tiers. Third parties have built businesses in the gaps (ScheduleReader, Adrega, OnePager, Outbuild, Planera, Aphex). Against that, we already deliver several of the longest-standing asks outright (section 2), and the gap diff shows the everyday work (draw, link, drag, progress, baseline, filter, colour, issue a PDF) is have or partial with S-sized gaps. The backlog below therefore leads with the output and evidence-trail items the earlier report also put first: a sheet that prints as the planner laid it out, a weekly lookahead as one view, a change log with reasons, and reports clients can read, before the deeper engine options.
