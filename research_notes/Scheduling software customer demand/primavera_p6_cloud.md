# Oracle Primavera P6 (Professional / EPPM) and Oracle Primavera Cloud: customer demand, complaints and pain points (construction planning focus)

Research date: 2026-11-17. Evidence quality note up front: Reddit could not be fetched (domain blocked to the fetch tool, and web search returned no Reddit threads). G2, Capterra, Software Advice and Gartner Peer Insights returned HTTP 403, so review-site material below comes from search-result snippets, not full pages. Oracle's Primavera "Ideas" (enhancement request) portal sits inside My Oracle Support (login required), so **no vote counts could be retrieved**. Frequency signals below are therefore counts of independent sources/posters found, not portal votes.

## 1. What do P6 users repeatedly complain about?

### Takeaway
The complaints that recur across review sites, forums and trainer blogs are: steep learning curve / complexity, a dated and cluttered UI, high cost (licence plus training), weak reporting and printing (no native PDF export, Gantt dropping out of prints, page breaks), slow or poor vendor support and long-lived bugs, and gaps between the P6 web client (EPPM) and the desktop client. Much of the forum evidence is older (2008-2020), but the same themes appear in 2025-2026 review aggregates, which suggests they have not been solved.

### Cited Findings
**Ratings (frequency/scale signal)**
- G2 and Capterra both rate Oracle Primavera about 4.4/5. One G2 comparison page shows 385 reviews and another summary 391; counts vary between pages — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/oracle-primavera-p6-vs-procore); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/145503/Oracle-Primavera/reviews/) (search snippet; full pages returned 403)
- Gartner Peer Insights (P6 EPPM): 4.6 from 70 ratings. Recurring dislikes in snippets: "slowness and complexity of process, too many EBS levels, calculations, etc." and "terrible implementation and customer support" — [Gartner Peer Insights](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/project-portfolio-management-worldwide/vendor/oracle/product/primavera-enterprise-project-portfolio-management) (snippet only)

**Learning curve / complexity (most-repeated theme; seen on Capterra, G2, Software Advice, Gartner, trainer guides and Planning Planet)**
- Capterra reviewer: P6 is "very possibly the least easy-to-use program I've ever been taught" — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/145503/Oracle-Primavera/reviews/)
- One review guide says many users report it takes months of regular use or formal training before they are confident — [ITQlick](https://www.itqlick.com/primavera-p6) (SEO-style aggregator, low weight)
- OLDER (2008) Planning Planet thread "What is the biggest problem in P6": 3 posters raise complexity ("so damned complicated to use it takes most people a few years to understand"; "The added complexity of products like P5/P6 have done nothing to improve planner productivity"), and 3 say lack of training is the real problem — [Planning Planet / projectcontrols.com](https://projectcontrols.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/417788/what-biggest-problem-p6)
- Gartner snippet: "slowness and complexity of process" — [Gartner Peer Insights](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/project-portfolio-management-worldwide/vendor/oracle/product/primavera-enterprise-project-portfolio-management)

**Dated / cluttered UI**
- Capterra reviewer: "the software UI is old style and the options are not properly organised" — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/145503/Oracle-Primavera/reviews/)
- OLDER Planning Planet posts: interface "cramped, too many icons"; P6 harder than P3 for everyday filtering; an "annoying habit of losing layouts" — [Planning Planet "P6 more easy options"](https://planningplanet.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/419672/p6-more-easy-options); [P3 versus P6](https://planningplanet.com/forums/planning-scheduling-programming-discussion/418139/p3-versus-p6-what-are-your-views)
- Oracle's on-prem EPPM 25.12 release is described as bringing "Redwood icons and visual updates", i.e. Oracle is still working on visual modernisation — [Ten Six, EPPM 25.12](https://tensix.com/primavera-p6-eppm-25-12-is-released/)
- P6 Professional is Windows-only; Mac users need a VM or the web EPPM edition — [ProjectManager comparison](https://www.projectmanager.com/blog/primavera-p6-vs-microsoft-project) (vendor of a competing tool; low weight)

**Cost**
- G2 reviewer: "costly to own and train your people on it" — [G2 pros and cons](https://www.g2.com/products/oracle-primavera/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons) (snippet)
- Claimed 2021 price rises: P6 Cloud licence +100%, PPM/EPPM about +30%; P6 Pro listed at $3,200 in 2021. The source is a Powerproject reseller and cites no evidence for the figures — [Petroglyph Projects (competitor reseller, 2021)](https://www.petroglyphprojects.com/blog/the-oracle-primavera-p6-price-increase-of-2021-leaves-users-searching-for-alternatives/)
- Support renewals are said to be about 20-22% of licence cost a year; one licensing consultancy says moving P6 to Primavera Cloud can swap a capped 6% renewal increase for an uncapped one and add 15-30% to first-year cost — [Redress Compliance](https://redresscompliance.com/oracle-primavera-cloud-vs-p6-eppm-licensing) (advisory firm with a commercial angle)
- OLDER: licence protection makes moving a licence between PCs hard; "I cannot afford to buy P6" — [Planning Planet](https://projectcontrols.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/417788/what-biggest-problem-p6)

**Bugs and support**
- Capterra reviewer: "there are some software bugs that are still persistent and oracle has been very slow in debugging them" — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/145503/Oracle-Primavera/reviews/)
- Gartner snippet: "terrible implementation and customer support" — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/project-portfolio-management-worldwide/vendor/oracle/product/primavera-enterprise-project-portfolio-management)
- G2 summary: reviewers note it "sometimes struggles with configuration challenges" — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/oracle-primavera-p6-vs-procore)

**Reporting, printing and layouts (repeated across 4+ independent sources)**
- No native "save as PDF" in P6 Professional: "Oracle haven't gotten around to adding support for saving a schedule in PDF yet", so users need a PDF printer driver — [Plan Academy](https://www.planacademy.com/print-to-pdf-primavera-p6-professional/) (about 2025)
- Planning Planet: Gantt charts stopped appearing in page layout and prints (about 2018) — [Planning Planet](https://planningplanet.com/forums/oracle-primavera-pm6/667358/p6-not-printing-gantt-chart-along-table); on an 800-page schedule, row-height optimisation left blank gaps and broke pages (about 2020) — [Planning Planet](https://planningplanet.com/forums/oracle-primavera-pm6/875982/p6-breaks-page-while-printing-pdf)
- OLDER (2008): "release an expensive product without any quality, in-built graphic reporting capability"; "I can't even print a S curve showing Baseline vs Planned in man hours" — [Planning Planet](https://projectcontrols.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/417788/what-biggest-problem-p6)
- Primavera Cloud, by contrast, has a built-in print-to-PDF workflow with preview, but it needs "Enhanced Printing" turned on and browser background graphics on for Gantt colours — [Oracle OPC docs](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E80480_01/English/user_guides/schedule_management_user_guide/164138.htm)

**XER / data exchange**
- XER export does not carry baselines: "In the past, exporting a project to XER meant losing your baselines" (Plan Academy, framing a 25.12 fix) — [Plan Academy 25.12](https://www.planacademy.com/whats-new-in-primavera-p6-version-25-12/); Oracle's docs confirm P6 Professional "does not support exporting project baselines in XER file format" — [Oracle import/export guide](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E80480_01/English/admin/p6_import_guide/190894.htm)
- Primavera Cloud ignores P6 Professional layouts on import ("does not support import of P6 Professional layouts") — [Oracle P6 to OPC import doc](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E80480_01/English/admin/p6_import_guide/190894.htm)
- A thriving third-party XER viewer market (e.g. ScheduleReader) exists because reviewers and clients need to open XERs without a P6 licence; a G2 review of ScheduleReader notes its custom filtering "is not as powerful as it is in P6" — [G2 ScheduleReader](https://www.g2.com/products/schedulereader/reviews?page=1)

**Undo (long-standing pain)**
- P6 Professional keeps up to 50 undo steps, but many actions clear the history: adding a scenario, applying view settings, assigning resources/roles, deleting objects, and (per an older guide) summarising, Update Progress, Apply Actuals, refresh and creating projects — [GlobalPM, "What can and can't you undo in P6"](https://globalpm.com/what-can-cant-you-undo-in-p6/); [PSTT-009 Undo tips PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5911985eebbd1a1c714927d5/t/6422eed0ae8f465c30fe0462/1680010961992/PSTT-009+Using+Undo+in+Oracle+Primavera+P6.pdf) (2023). Trainers publishing "what can't you undo" guides shows that users keep hitting these limits.

**Web (EPPM) vs desktop (Professional) gaps**
- Features said to be missing from the web: bucket planning, the WBS screen, text reports; daily data was only recently added to EPPM — [Plan Academy Pro vs EPPM](https://www.planacademy.com/primavera-p6-pro-vs-eppm-differences/)
- Tracking layouts are desktop-only; web activity views cannot be exported like layouts; desktop reports can only be viewed in the client, while web reporting relies on BI Publisher; time-scaled logic diagrams (Visualizer) are desktop-only — [Prescience Tip 16](https://www.prescience.com.au/primavera-tips-and-tricks-tip-16-what-is-the-difference-between-primavera-p6-web-and-p6-professional/)
- A LinkedIn post by a Primavera educator titled "P6 EPPM Is Not A Replacement For Primavera P6 Professional" (OLDER, about 10 years) said web resource and capacity features were unreliable — [LinkedIn, Michael Lepage](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-print-pdf-primavera-p6-professional-michael-lepage-primavera-p6-1)
- Typical deployment: power planners stay on the Windows client while managers use web — [P6 Consulting](https://www.p6consulting.ca/blog/p6-windows-professional-versus-p6-windows-client-for-eppm/)

**Progress updating / percent complete**
- OLDER: P6 has three percent-complete types that "do not match unless an activity is complete", a frequent source of confusion — [Planning Planet tips thread](https://planningplanet.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/418580/tips-and-tricks-primavera-6)
- 25.12 added an out-of-sequence warning because P6 "hasn't been great at warning you when your updates might cause problems" (the trainer also says the alert detail is basic) — [Plan Academy 25.12](https://www.planacademy.com/whats-new-in-primavera-p6-version-25-12/)

**Performance and database administration**
- Gartner snippet: "slowness"; Oracle's 25.12 release added on-demand loading of large data objects (calendars, notes, notebook topics) to cut delays opening large projects, a configurable DB read timeout, and a new SQL logging tool. These are vendor-side signals that large-project performance and DB administration are pain points — [Ten Six P6 Pro 25.12](https://tensix.com/p6-professional-25-12-is-released/)
- OLDER (about 2014) forum post: "Unable to connect to the database" — [Planning Planet](https://projectcontrols.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/417788/what-biggest-problem-p6)

### Inferences
- Ranked by how often independent sources repeat them: (1) learning curve/complexity; (2) cost; (3) dated UI; (4) reporting/printing/PDF; (5) support and bugs; (6) web vs desktop gaps; (7) XER limits (baselines, layouts); (8) undo limits; (9) large-project performance. Items 7-9 come up more in trainer content and Oracle's own fixes than in review cons, which suggests they are power-user pain rather than buyer pain.
- Oracle's 2025 releases are mostly baseline, global-change and performance plumbing, not UI or ease-of-use work. The top review complaint (learning curve) remains unaddressed, which leaves room for a simpler planner-first tool that can still read and write XER.

### Gaps
- No full-text G2/Capterra/Gartner reviews (403), so no theme counts such as "Learning curve (n)" or reviewer roles and dates.
- No Reddit data at all. r/Primavera, r/ConstructionManagers and r/projectmanagement threads could not be found by search or fetched.
- Copy-paste and Excel round-tripping complaints were not found in sources this pass, so they are unverified.

## 2. Most-voted / most-repeated requests on Oracle Ideas and forums

### Takeaway
Oracle runs P6 enhancement requests through "Ideas" in the My Oracle Support Primavera community, which requires a login. No vote counts were retrievable. Repeated forum and trainer requests point to: better reporting/graphics and S-curves, native PDF output, fuller undo, keeping baselines in exports, less all-or-nothing bulk editing (Global Change), out-of-sequence warnings, resource-level earned value, and web/desktop parity. Several of these were addressed in 25.12.

### Cited Findings
- Oracle moved Primavera enhancement requests to "Ideas" in the MOS Primavera community, where users submit, vote on and follow ERs — [Oracle MOSC announcement](https://community.oracle.com/mosc/discussion/3699225/your-ideas-count-ideas-now-live-in-the-primavera-prim-community) (about 2015); FAQ at [Doc ID 1995605.1](https://support.oracle.com/knowledge/More%20Applications%20and%20Technologies/1995605_1.html) (login)
- Oracle's general Idea Lab policy: it prioritises "top-ranking ideas as indicated by your votes and comments" but gives no roadmap guarantee — [Oracle Idea Lab guidelines](https://community.oracle.com/customerconnect/categories/idealab-guidelines)
- An OPSIG (Oracle Primavera user group) "P6 enhancement requests" deck from 2011 exists (OLDER, not reviewed in detail) — [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/p6academy/2011-p6-enhancement-requests-opsig)
- OLDER (2008) Planning Planet "biggest problem": earned value at resource level, not just activity level (4 posters, the most-mentioned item in that thread); trustworthy calculations and resource levelling (3); reporting and graphing (3) — [Planning Planet](https://projectcontrols.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/417788/what-biggest-problem-p6)
- OLDER: users wanted to open two projects side by side as in P3; Oracle's answers in a P6 v7 Q&A deferred features such as a "zero total float" constraint and time-phased logic views on web to "long term development plans" — [Planning Planet "P6 Planning Software"](https://planningplanet.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/523287/p6-planning-software)
- Trainers frame 25.12 fixes as answers to long-standing limits: Global Change was "all-or-nothing"; XER lost baselines; P6 was weak at warning about bad updates — [Plan Academy 25.12](https://www.planacademy.com/whats-new-in-primavera-p6-version-25-12/)

### Inferences
- With no vote data, the best proxy for Ideas demand is what Oracle ships. In the 25.12 release, the heaviest investment went into baselines (5 separate items), which suggests baseline handling is a top request area.

### Gaps
- No vote counts for any P6 or OPC idea. Getting them needs a MOS login. Undo depth, Gantt drag-editing and copy-paste requests could not be tied to a specific Idea with votes.

## 3. What users say about Primavera Cloud (OPC)

### Takeaway
Little first-hand planner commentary on OPC turned up. What exists cites a steep learning curve, slow first load, support and upgrade friction, migration losses (layouts, privileges, resource curves, UDFs, some Gantt customisation), and pricing that is disputed: partners say it is cheaper, licensing advisers say it is dearer at scale. Oracle ships monthly OPC updates focused on filters, baseline variance, schedule health checks and task/field apps.

### Cited Findings
- OPC reviews: "the learning curve for Oracle Primavera Cloud to be a bit steep, particularly for new users"; "complex and not intuitive for non-experienced project managers"; "initial loading time is a bit lengthier"; one reviewer calls it "the most powerful planning and scheduling tool at the moment" — [Software Advice OPC reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/construction/primaverp6eppm-profile/reviews/); [Softwarefinder](https://softwarefinder.com/project-management-software/primavera-cloud) (snippets; small sample)
- Support responsiveness, upgrade friction and monthly maintenance windows come up in comparative reviews — [rfp.wiki](https://www.rfp.wiki/project-management/oracle-primavera/autodesk-construction-cloud) (automated aggregator, low confidence)
- Migration losses (Oracle docs): P6 privileges cannot be moved; OPC uses a "more simplified approach to codes"; saved views only partly supported and saved per user; some import items are "not supported" functionally — [Oracle P6 EPPM to OPC migration guide](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E80480_01/English/admin/p6_eppm_migration_guide/primavera_cloud_p6_eppm_migration.pdf); [Oracle P6 to OPC import guide](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E80480_01/English/admin/p6_import_guide/primavera_cloud_p6_import.pdf). P6 Professional layouts are ignored on import — [Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E80480_01/English/admin/p6_import_guide/190894.htm)
- Consultants warn that custom resource curves can silently reset to linear and that indicator/dropdown UDFs lose values unless defined identically; resource shifts, hourly spreads and some Gantt customisation don't carry over; migrations take 3-6 months for a mid-sized group — [CMC Project Solutions](https://www.cmcprojectsolutions.com/posts/transitioning-from-oracle-primavera-p6-to-oracle-primavera-cloud/); [Compass Consult](https://compassconsult.co/oracle-primavera-cloud-vs-p6/) (secondary). A reader comment claims OPC caps projects at 10,000 tasks versus 100,000 in P6 Pro, and that P6 Pro has multiple float paths. UNVERIFIED against Oracle docs.
- Pricing conflict: an implementation partner says OPC subscription is "typically around one-third the cost" of a comparable P6 licence — [Taradigm / partner guide](https://www.taradigm.com/oracle-primavera-cloud-vs-primavera-p6-9-advantages-of-oracle-primavera-cloud/); a licensing adviser says OPC adds 15-30% to first-year cost — [Redress Compliance](https://redresscompliance.com/oracle-primavera-cloud-vs-p6-eppm-licensing). Both have commercial interests.
- Recent OPC releases (third-party summaries, 2026): **26.9**: multi-select "is one of / is not one of" filters on schedule codes; baseline start/finish variance in charts — [Equivtech 26.9](https://www.equivtech.com.au/Primavera-Cloud-New-Update-26-9.html). **26.6** (June 2026): workspace-level configurable schedule health check pushed to child workspaces; drag activities into grouping bands; find/replace in task list — [Emerald Associates 26.6](https://www.emerald-associates.com/whats-new-in-oracle-primavera-cloud-opc-v26-6-2026-06-25.html?calid=274). **26.4**: default health-check settings inherited by new projects — [Emerald 26.4](https://www.emerald-associates.com/item/oracle-primavera-cloud-opc-v26-4-whats-new.html). **26.2**: baseline duration/units/cost variance in activity chart filters — [Equivtech 26.2](https://www.equivtech.com.au/Primavera-Cloud-New-Update-26-2.html) (the search snippet dated 26.2 as "July 2026", which conflicts with its version number; date unverified)
- The OPC Task app targets field/lean construction planning (task boards for foremen) — [GlobalPM](https://globalpm.com/how-the-oracle-primavera-cloud-task-application-transforms-construction-planning/) (partner marketing)

### Inferences
- OPC is moving demand toward built-in schedule quality (health checks set centrally), easier filtering, and baseline-variance visualisation. These are the same things planners now buy Acumen/Fuse or ScheduleReader for on P6.
- Migration friction (layouts, codes, UDFs, curves) suggests a tool that converts XER/P6 data faithfully, layouts included, would meet a real need.

### Gaps
- No first-hand planner forum or Reddit accounts of OPC adoption, and no evidence of a "forced migration" (none found). The 10k-task cap is unverified.

## 4. Scheduling-engine / methodology gaps raised by experienced planners

### Takeaway
The recurring expert debates are out-of-sequence progress handling (retained logic vs progress override vs actual dates), longest path vs total-float criticality, mixed-calendar effects on float, percent-complete types, resource levelling reliability, and earned value granularity. Experts mostly treat these as traps P6 lets users fall into rather than missing features; Oracle's 25.12 out-of-sequence warning and calendar detail in the scheduling log respond to some of them.

### Cited Findings
- Retained logic generally gives the longest critical path, while progress override "invariably produces a shorter critical path" and can leave illogical gaps — [Planning Planet "Retained logic & Progress override"](https://planningplanet.com/forums/planning-scheduling-programming-discussion/413516/retained-logic-progress-override); override "can make the project look more optimistic" — [P6 Consulting](https://www.p6consulting.ca/blog/progress-override-vs-retained-logic-which-to-use-and-what-to-look-out-for/)
- The "Actual Dates" option acts as retained logic while the out-of-sequence activity is in progress and as override once it finishes, a non-obvious hybrid — [Consult Leopard](https://consultleopard.com/retained-logic-vs-progress-override-vs-actual-dates-scheduling-progress-settings-in-primavera-p6-professional/); see also [Ten Six out-of-sequence options](https://tensix.com/options-for-handling-out-of-sequence-progress-in-primavera-p6/), [Eastwood Harris schedule options](https://eastwoodharris.com/DL/TP/190416_Understanding_P6_Schedule_Options_When_scheduling_activities_use.pdf), [PM World Journal "Retain or Override" (2017, OLDER)](https://pmworldlibrary.net/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/pmwj62-Sep2017-Paterson-retain-or-override-featured-paper.pdf)
- Experts advise reviewing the longest path and near-critical paths after every update instead of relying on a total-float threshold; Ron Winter's "Inner Workings of P6" documents engine behaviour in detail — [Ron Winter](https://www.ronwinterconsulting.com/The_Inner_Workings_Of_P6.pdf); [Proove](https://www.proove.eu/knowledge/scheduling-progressed-activities)
- Mixed calendars: 25.12's scheduling/levelling log now names the calendars, predecessors and projects when "Finish milestone and predecessors have different calendars", a sign that calendar-driven float distortions are a support burden — [Ten Six 25.12](https://tensix.com/p6-professional-25-12-is-released/)
- Out-of-sequence alert (25.12) is optional (User Preferences > Assistance) and does not fire for Apply Actuals or Update Progress — [Ten Six 25.12](https://tensix.com/p6-professional-25-12-is-released/)
- OLDER (2008): "CAN'T EVEN PERFORM THE BASIC FUNCTIONS OF RESOURCE LEVELLING"; "50,000 different combinations of calculating the same data and get a different answer"; want "trustworthy forward/backward pass calculations"; EV at resource level — [Planning Planet](https://projectcontrols.com/forums/primavera-version-pm5-pm6/417788/what-biggest-problem-p6)
- New 25.12 Estimate-to-Complete fields (original duration divided by SPI) show demand for forecast metrics built into the schedule — [Ten Six 25.12](https://tensix.com/p6-professional-25-12-is-released/)

### Inferences
- The demand is for guardrails and transparency, such as warnings, explanations of why a date moved, and visible driving paths, more than for new algorithms. A tool that defaults to retained logic, flags out-of-sequence progress, explains calendar effects and shows longest/driving paths would answer the experts' concerns.

### Gaps
- No user evidence was found this pass on multiple float paths, risk (P6 lacks native Monte Carlo; Oracle Primavera Risk Analysis is separate) or lag misuse. These are known topics but were not sourced here.

## 5. UK/European construction planners and clients

### Takeaway
In the UK, P6 is required by contract scope rather than by NEC itself. When the Scope names P6 or XER, submitting in another tool can be grounds for rejecting the programme. Clients and PMs want native files (XER) so they can check logic and float. DCMA 14-point checks (often run in Acumen Fuse on the XER, or P6's Check Schedule) are common practice but, per one UK source, not mandated on UK programmes.

### Cited Findings
- NEC4 clause 31.3 lets the Project Manager reject a programme that doesn't comply with the Scope, which can specify software, format and level of detail. If the Scope requires P6 and the contractor submits MS Project, that is grounds for rejection — [NEC Planning Solutions](https://www.necplanningsolutions.co.uk/post/primavera-p6-for-nec-programmes); [Gather Insights NEC4](https://www.gatherinsights.com/en/nec4/programme-management); [Ramskill Martin NEC4 compliance](https://ramskillmartin.co.uk/technical/article-58-nec4-june-2017-programme-compliance)
- Native files over PDFs are recommended so the PM can interrogate logic and float; revised programmes are due at intervals in Contract Data (typically 4 weeks); the PM has 2 weeks to respond — [NEC Planning Solutions, PM review checks](https://www.necplanningsolutions.co.uk/post/what-project-manager-checks-nec-programme-review)
- UK job ads ask for "hands on proficiency and experience with Primavera P6" — [DWP Find a Job](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/16262940)
- "Is DCMA 14 mandated on UK programmes? No"; recent P6 Pro releases include a Check Schedule report covering most checks, but its default thresholds need aligning to DCMA — [SOMA Project Controls](https://www.somaprojectcontrols.com/resources/guides/dcma-14-point-assessment-uk/)
- Acumen Fuse imports XERs (several schedules per XER) for DCMA checks; Steelray and ScheduleReader are alternatives — [Ten Six Acumen/DCMA](https://tensix.com/deltek-acumen-fuse-and-the-dcmas-14-point-assessment/); [Plan Academy Acumen](https://www.planacademy.com/deltek-acumen-fuse-schedule-check/)
- DCMA thresholds most often quoted: lags under 5% of relationships, at least 90% FS relationships, BEI at least 0.95, no negative lags — [SOMA](https://www.somaprojectcontrols.com/resources/guides/dcma-14-point-assessment-uk/); [Proove (EU)](https://www.proove.eu/knowledge/the-14-point-dcma-schedule-assessment)
- Oracle documents NEC4 contract workflows in Unifier (not P6), showing UK contract demand at the portfolio/contract layer — [Oracle Unifier NEC4 guide](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/F88970_01/English/NEC4/NEC4_contractor/NEC4_user_contractor.pdf)

### Inferences
- For UK construction, XER import/export fidelity and built-in schedule quality checks (DCMA-style with configurable thresholds) carry as much weight as P6 parity itself. A non-P6 tool will be rejected where the Scope names P6 unless it exports clean XER.

### Gaps
