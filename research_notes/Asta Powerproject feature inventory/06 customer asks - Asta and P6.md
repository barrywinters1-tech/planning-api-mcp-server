# 06 Customer asks - Asta Powerproject and Oracle Primavera P6

What customers of Asta Powerproject (Elecosoft/Eleco) and Oracle Primavera P6 have asked for, complained about, or worked around for years. Compiled 21 Nov 2026.

## Method note

- 38 distinct web searches and about 45 page fetches. Sources that yielded usable material: Planning Planet (now redirects to projectcontrols.com; the Asta forum there has 403 topics, the P6 forums many more), G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, TrustRadius, SelectHub, SaaSworthy, PeerSpot (via search snippets), Oracle My Oracle Support Community (MOSC) and Oracle Support knowledge articles (via snippets; most MOSC pages returned 503 or need login), Oracle and Elecosoft release notes and help, Ten Six, ScheduleReader, Planera and ProjectManager.com (competitor blogs, flagged as such), ENR, PBC Today, Construction Dive, the 2005 PEO software survey thread, and a 2011 OPSIG enhancement-request deck (title only; slide body would not load).
- Not reachable: Reddit is blocked for this crawler (both search and fetch refused), so no r/projectmanagement, r/construction or r/PrimaveraP6 evidence is included. YouTube comment threads are not indexed by the search tool. LinkedIn posts did not surface in any search. G2, Capterra, Gartner Peer Insights, Software Advice and GetApp pages returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch, so review-site evidence comes from search-engine snippets of those pages and is thinner than it should be. The Elecosoft forum/ideas board does not appear to exist publicly; Elecosoft collects feedback through a telemetry "Product Improvement Programme" (v16) and customer meetings.
- "Count" is the number of distinct sources (threads, reviews, articles) in which I saw the ask. "Earliest" is the earliest dated mention I found, not necessarily the first time it was ever raised. Where a Planning Planet reply shows only "N years ago", the thread creation date is used.
- Quotation marks are used only for text copied exactly from a source; everything else is paraphrased.
- Vendor status: unaddressed / partly / addressed (version). Judged against P6 Professional 24.x-25.x and Asta Powerproject 17 / 2026.1-2026.3 release notes.

Row count: 100 (tables below).

- Asta complaints are fewer and more specific (progress periods, baselines, transfer fidelity, large-file stability) and the vendor has shipped against several (autosave, timeline, Vision Live, 2026.1 UX items), but the as-built/baseline problem has had no named fix since 2011 and there is no public ideas board.
